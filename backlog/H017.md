# H017 — Goal-vs-time weekly review

## Related JTBD
`time-feedback`

## Problem statement
Users lack an objective comparison between priorities and actual time use.

## Evidence
Synthetic CustDev fixtures show high importance and low satisfaction for objective time-use feedback. This evidence is synthetic and must be replaced or corroborated with real interviews.

## Assumption
A weekly goal-vs-time report exposes misalignment and prompts corrective action.

## Proposed experiment
Generate synthetic weekly activity summaries and test actionability ratings.
