# H015 — Alternative generation with constraints

## Related JTBD
`decision-support`

## Problem statement
Users often generate options that violate real constraints.

## Evidence
Synthetic CustDev fixtures show high importance and low satisfaction for making evidence-backed decisions. This evidence is synthetic and must be replaced or corroborated with real interviews.

## Assumption
Constraint-aware alternatives improve relevance and reduce option overload.

## Proposed experiment
Collect constraints first, then compare constrained vs unconstrained alternative generation.
