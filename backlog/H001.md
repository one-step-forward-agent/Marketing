# H001 — Automatic task context recap

## Related JTBD
`context-restoration`

## Problem statement
Users returning to a task need a concise reconstruction of prior state and next step.

## Evidence
Synthetic CustDev fixtures show high importance and low satisfaction for restoring lost context. This evidence is synthetic and must be replaced or corroborated with real interviews.

## Assumption
A source-linked recap can reduce manual reconstruction time.

## Proposed experiment
Prototype a recap view generated from synthetic activity history and run 5 usability sessions.

## Expected impact
10/10

## Implementation complexity
5/10

## Confidence
0.85

## Priority score
1.700

Formula: `(Expected Impact × Confidence) / Implementation Complexity`.

## Success metric
Median time-to-resume is at least 30% lower than control.

## Kill criterion
Kill if median time-to-resume improves by <10% or users cannot trace key claims.

## Dependencies
None for discovery experiment; product implementation depends on event/data-lineage infrastructure.
