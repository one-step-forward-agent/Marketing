# H019 — Attention fragmentation score

## Related JTBD
`time-feedback`

## Problem statement
Frequent switching may explain weak progress better than total hours.

## Evidence
Synthetic CustDev fixtures show high importance and low satisfaction for objective time-use feedback. This evidence is synthetic and must be replaced or corroborated with real interviews.

## Assumption
A transparent fragmentation metric helps users identify disruptive patterns.

## Proposed experiment
Compare a simple switch-rate metric against self-reported focus quality in test sessions.

## Expected impact

## Implementation complexity

## Confidence

## Priority score

Formula: `(Expected Impact × Confidence) / Implementation Complexity`.

## Success metric
Metric direction agrees with user-rated fragmentation in >=70% of sessions.

## Kill criterion
Kill if users interpret the score as productivity quality despite explanations.
