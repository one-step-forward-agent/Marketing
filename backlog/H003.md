# H003 — Next meaningful step suggestion

## Related JTBD
`context-restoration`

## Problem statement
Users reconstruct context mainly to decide what to do next.

## Evidence
Synthetic CustDev fixtures show high importance and low satisfaction for restoring lost context. This evidence is synthetic and must be replaced or corroborated with real interviews.

## Assumption
A next-step suggestion grounded in recent events shortens restart friction.

## Proposed experiment
Compare manual restart versus generated next-step suggestion in timed tasks.
