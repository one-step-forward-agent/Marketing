# H021 — Deep-work evidence window

## Related JTBD

## Problem statement
Users want objective evidence of sustained focus, not only time totals.

## Evidence
Synthetic CustDev fixtures show high importance and low satisfaction for objective time-use feedback. This evidence is synthetic and must be replaced or corroborated with real interviews.

## Assumption
Detecting uninterrupted work windows makes focus patterns visible.

## Proposed experiment
Derive candidate deep-work windows from synthetic events and ask users to validate them.
