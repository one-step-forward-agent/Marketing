# H008 — Selective context forgetting

## Related JTBD
`context-restoration`

## Problem statement
Too much historical context can become noise.

## Evidence
Synthetic CustDev fixtures show high importance and low satisfaction for restoring lost context. This evidence is synthetic and must be replaced or corroborated with real interviews.

## Assumption
User-controlled expiry and pinning improve relevance of reconstructed context.

## Proposed experiment
Prototype pin/expire controls and run ranking tasks on long synthetic histories.
