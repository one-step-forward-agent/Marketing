# H020 — Priority drift alerts

## Related JTBD
`time-feedback`

## Problem statement
Time allocation can drift away from declared priorities during the week.

## Evidence
Synthetic CustDev fixtures show high importance and low satisfaction for objective time-use feedback. This evidence is synthetic and must be replaced or corroborated with real interviews.

## Assumption
Low-frequency drift alerts enable correction before weekly review.

## Proposed experiment
Simulate threshold-based alerts with adjustable sensitivity.

## Expected impact
8/10

## Implementation complexity
5/10

## Confidence
0.70

## Priority score
1.120

Formula: `(Expected Impact × Confidence) / Implementation Complexity`.

## Success metric
At least 50% of alerts cause a deliberate reprioritization and false-positive complaints stay <20%.

## Kill criterion
Kill if alert dismissal exceeds 80%.

## Dependencies
None for discovery experiment; product implementation depends on event/data-lineage infrastructure.
