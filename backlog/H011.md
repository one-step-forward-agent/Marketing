# H011 — Reversible decision mode

## Related JTBD
`decision-support`

## Problem statement
Fear of irreversible choices contributes to delay.

## Evidence
Synthetic CustDev fixtures show high importance and low satisfaction for making evidence-backed decisions. This evidence is synthetic and must be replaced or corroborated with real interviews.

## Assumption
Framing reversible decisions with checkpoints reduces unnecessary analysis.

## Proposed experiment
Classify sample decisions by reversibility and test suggested checkpoint plans.
