# H009 — Explicit decision criteria matrix

## Related JTBD
`decision-support`

## Problem statement
Decision paralysis is amplified when criteria remain implicit.

## Evidence
Synthetic CustDev fixtures show high importance and low satisfaction for making evidence-backed decisions. This evidence is synthetic and must be replaced or corroborated with real interviews.

## Assumption
Making criteria explicit and weighted reduces indecision.

## Proposed experiment
Compare free-form AI advice with a criteria matrix on three ambiguous decisions.

## Expected impact
10/10

## Implementation complexity
4/10

## Confidence

## Priority score
2.150

Formula: `(Expected Impact × Confidence) / Implementation Complexity`.

## Success metric
Decision completion time falls by 25% and confidence rises without lower consistency.

## Kill criterion
Kill if completion time improves by <10% or users reject weighting as burdensome.
