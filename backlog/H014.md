# H014 — Decision deadline guardrail

## Related JTBD
`decision-support`

## Problem statement
Analysis expands when there is no explicit stop condition.

## Evidence
Synthetic CustDev fixtures show high importance and low satisfaction for making evidence-backed decisions. This evidence is synthetic and must be replaced or corroborated with real interviews.

## Assumption
A user-defined decision deadline and evidence threshold reduces rumination.

## Proposed experiment
Test decision sessions with explicit stop rules versus open-ended exploration.
