# H022 — Meeting cost reflection

## Related JTBD
`time-feedback`

## Problem statement
Meeting volume can consume time without visible goal contribution.

## Evidence
Synthetic CustDev fixtures show high importance and low satisfaction for objective time-use feedback. This evidence is synthetic and must be replaced or corroborated with real interviews.

## Assumption
Linking meetings to goals and follow-up outcomes improves meeting hygiene.

## Proposed experiment
Prototype a weekly meeting contribution report with goal links.

## Success metric

## Kill criterion
