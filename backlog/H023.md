# H023 — Behavioral baseline and trend

## Related JTBD
`time-feedback`

## Problem statement
Single-week data is noisy.

## Evidence
Synthetic CustDev fixtures show high importance and low satisfaction for objective time-use feedback. This evidence is synthetic and must be replaced or corroborated with real interviews.

## Assumption
A personal baseline helps distinguish meaningful behavior changes from normal variance.

## Proposed experiment
Generate four weeks of synthetic activity and test trend explanations.
