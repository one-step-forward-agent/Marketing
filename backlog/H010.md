# H010 — Evidence-backed recommendation

## Related JTBD
`decision-support`

## Problem statement
Generic recommendations are hard to trust.

## Evidence
Synthetic CustDev fixtures show high importance and low satisfaction for making evidence-backed decisions. This evidence is synthetic and must be replaced or corroborated with real interviews.

## Assumption
Showing evidence for and against each option improves decision quality.

## Proposed experiment
Prototype recommendation cards with source-linked arguments and counterarguments.

## Expected impact
9/10

## Implementation complexity
5/10

## Confidence
0.84

## Priority score
1.512

Formula: `(Expected Impact × Confidence) / Implementation Complexity`.

## Success metric
At least 75% can identify why the recommended option won and cite supporting evidence.

## Kill criterion
Kill if explanation comprehension is <60% or recommendation acceptance becomes blind.

## Dependencies
None for discovery experiment; product implementation depends on event/data-lineage infrastructure.
