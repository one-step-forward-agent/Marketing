# H005 — Context snapshot on task switch

## Related JTBD
`context-restoration`

## Problem statement
Repeated switching creates predictable context loss.

## Evidence
Synthetic CustDev fixtures show high importance and low satisfaction for restoring lost context. This evidence is synthetic and must be replaced or corroborated with real interviews.

## Assumption
Capturing a lightweight snapshot at switch time improves later restoration.

## Proposed experiment
Simulate task switches and compare automatic snapshot vs no snapshot.
