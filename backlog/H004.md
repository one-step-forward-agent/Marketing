# H004 — Cross-source context timeline

## Related JTBD
`context-restoration`

## Problem statement
Context is fragmented across calendar, notes, tasks and chats.

## Evidence
Synthetic CustDev fixtures show high importance and low satisfaction for restoring lost context. This evidence is synthetic and must be replaced or corroborated with real interviews.

## Assumption
A unified timeline reveals causal sequence better than per-tool search.

## Proposed experiment
Build a clickable timeline using synthetic events from four source types.

## Implementation complexity

## Success metric
70% of participants locate the relevant prior event in under 60 seconds.

## Kill criterion
