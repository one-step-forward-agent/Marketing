# H012 — Decision journal with outcome review

## Related JTBD
`decision-support`

## Problem statement
Users rarely compare decisions with eventual outcomes.

## Evidence
Synthetic CustDev fixtures show high importance and low satisfaction for making evidence-backed decisions. This evidence is synthetic and must be replaced or corroborated with real interviews.

## Assumption
Closing the loop improves calibration and future criteria selection.

## Proposed experiment
Create decision journal entries and schedule synthetic follow-up outcome reviews.

## Success metric
