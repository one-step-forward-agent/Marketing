# H002 — Source-linked decision history

## Related JTBD
`context-restoration`

## Problem statement
Users lose why a decision was made when evidence is scattered.

## Evidence
Synthetic CustDev fixtures show high importance and low satisfaction for restoring lost context. This evidence is synthetic and must be replaced or corroborated with real interviews.

## Assumption
A chronological decision record with source links improves trust and recall.

## Proposed experiment
Test a decision-history prototype with hidden/visible rationale variants.

## Expected impact
9/10

## Implementation complexity
4/10

## Confidence
0.82

## Priority score

Formula: `(Expected Impact × Confidence) / Implementation Complexity`.

## Success metric
At least 80% of participants correctly explain prior rationale after a 48h gap.

## Kill criterion
Kill if source links are ignored by >70% or recall is not better than notes.

None for discovery experiment; product implementation depends on event/data-lineage infrastructure.
