## Editorial scoring

Score each candidate 1–5 on:

- problem proximity;
- evidence availability;
- audience specificity;
- searchable demand;
- shareability;
- natural product bridge;
- production effort.

Prioritize high problem/evidence/bridge with low-medium effort. Do not publish broad "AI productivity" content merely to fill a calendar.

## Content quality checklist

Before publishing:

1. Is the target segment explicit?
2. Is the trigger/problem concrete?
3. Are factual claims sourced?
4. Are customer quotes real and consented?
5. Is synthetic/internal data labeled or excluded?
6. Does the content teach something useful without requiring the product?
7. Is there one natural CTA aligned with current validation stage?
8. Can a reader distinguish evidence from opinion?
