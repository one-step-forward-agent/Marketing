# Content strategy v0.1

## Objective
Use content to recruit research participants, test problem language, and build credibility around evidence-backed work — not to manufacture broad top-of-funnel volume before positioning is validated.

## Searchable vs. shareable

### Searchable problem clusters (keyword demand must be validated separately)
1. **How to restore context after interruptions**
   - returning to a project after a break
   - context switching recovery
   - decision log / decision journal
2. **How to make better work decisions**
   - decision criteria template
   - evidence-based decision making
   - reversible vs irreversible decisions
3. **How to audit where work time went**
   - weekly time review
   - calendar vs actual work
   - attention fragmentation

### Shareable content
- anonymized/aggregate "context reconstruction tax" findings once real interviews exist;
- teardown of one decision showing source→criteria→choice→outcome lineage;
- weekly "priority drift" visual based on opt-in user data;
- experiments comparing subjective vs observed time allocation.

## Buyer-stage mapping

| Stage | Question | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | "Why do I lose so much time after switching?" | research insight, diagnostic article |
| Consideration | "Can anything reconstruct the thread for me?" | workflow demo, evidence-lineage example |
| Decision | "Why trust this AI with my work history?" | privacy model, provenance explainer, concierge case study |
| Adoption | "How do I get value without more admin?" | setup guide, weekly review template |

## Editorial rule
Never turn synthetic fixture language into fake testimonials or "research says" content. Publish only real findings with source/sample disclosure.
