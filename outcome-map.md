# JTBD outcome map v0.1

## JTBD 1 — Restore context

**Situation:** returning after interruption, switching projects, or receiving new information.  
**Functional job:** reconstruct facts, previous decisions, open loops and the next meaningful step.  
**Emotional job:** regain confidence without the "I lost the thread" feeling.  
**Social job:** re-enter collaboration prepared instead of asking others to reconstruct history.  
**Desired outcome hypothesis:** reduce time-to-resume while preserving source traceability and uncertainty.

Candidate measurable outcomes:
- minutes from return to first meaningful action;
- % recap statements with inspectable source;
- correction rate / unsupported statement rate;
- frequency of repeated manual search across the same sources.

## JTBD 2 — Make a decision with evidence

**Situation:** several plausible options compete and criteria are implicit or changing.  
**Functional job:** compare alternatives against explicit criteria/evidence and commit to a reversible or irreversible choice.  
**Emotional job:** reduce analysis fatigue without surrendering agency.  
**Social job:** explain why the decision was made to stakeholders.  
**Desired outcome hypothesis:** shorten decision setup and preserve rationale that remains understandable later.

Candidate measurable outcomes:
- time to explicit criteria;
- option-to-decision conversion in a live case;
- % recommendations rejected due to unsupported evidence;
- rationale revisit rate within 14/30 days.

## JTBD 3 — Get objective time-use feedback

**Situation:** weekly review or low-progress moment where subjective busyness conflicts with priorities.  
**Functional job:** compare observed attention allocation with declared goals and understand drift.  
**Emotional job:** replace guilt/guessing with calm, explainable feedback.  
**Social job:** communicate capacity and trade-offs with evidence.  
**Desired outcome hypothesis:** help users change the following week's plan based on explainable evidence.

Candidate measurable outcomes:
- % activity confidently classified;
- minutes of weekly review overhead;
- goal-vs-time delta recognized by the user;
- week-2 / week-4 review return rate;
- number of next-week plan changes attributed to the review.
