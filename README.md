## Research branches

- `CustomerDevelopment` — structured CustDev fixtures, evidence matrix, research-quality controls and next-interview plan.
- `JobesToBeDone` — reproducible JTBD opportunity analysis, outcome map and candidate USP visualizations. The spelling is intentionally preserved from the project requirement.
- `Hypotheses` — prioritized Product Backlog, experiment design, HLA v0.1, data flows, and ADRs.

## Research integrity

The initial 30-interview corpus is synthetic test data created to validate the research and analytics pipeline. It is **not empirical customer evidence** and must never be presented as real transcripts, testimonials, market prevalence, product-market fit, or scientific proof of demand.

The marketing strategy therefore distinguishes:

`synthetic fixture → assumption → real interview/behavior evidence → validated insight → message/product decision`
