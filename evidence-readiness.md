# Evidence readiness

| JTBD | Synthetic fixtures | Empirical sources | Evidence grade | Can use for external market claim? |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| context-restoration | 10 | 0 | synthetic-only | No |
| decision-support | 10 | 0 | synthetic-only | No |
| time-feedback | 10 | 0 | synthetic-only | No |

## Promotion rule
A JTBD may move from a fixture hypothesis to a marketing insight only after source IDs point to real evidence and the research log documents segment, trigger, source type and contradictions.

## Minimum validation target
Before writing persona-level positioning from a job, collect at least 5 independent real episodes in one coherent segment and include negative/contradictory cases. This threshold is a practical research guardrail, not a statistical guarantee.
