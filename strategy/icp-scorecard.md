# ICP validation scorecard v0.1

**Status:** hypothesis-only until real interviews replace the synthetic corpus.

## Purpose

This scorecard prevents a broad "knowledge worker" persona from becoming the default audience. A segment earns priority only when it demonstrates a repeatable trigger, costly workaround, measurable outcome, reachable distribution channel and willingness to change behaviour.

## Candidate segments

| Segment | Context-switch intensity | Decision load | Existing workaround | Easy to recruit | Data-access feasibility | Current confidence |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Product managers / product leads | 5 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | Low — hypothesis |
| Startup founders / operators | 5 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | Low — hypothesis |
| Consultants / fractional leaders | 5 | 4 | 5 | 3 | 4 | Low — hypothesis |
| Engineering / team leads | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3 | Low — hypothesis |
| General knowledge workers | 3 | 3 | 3 | 5 | 3 | Too broad — deprioritized |

Scores are planning heuristics, not research results.

## Segment qualification questions

A segment becomes a beachhead candidate only if real evidence answers "yes" to most of the following:

1. Does the target person experience the job at least weekly?
2. Can they describe a recent concrete episode without prompting?
3. Does the episode create a measurable cost: time, delay, rework, missed commitment, stress or poor decision quality?
4. Is there an observable workaround already in use?
5. Is switching triggered by a recognizable situation?
6. Can the user adopt the product without a long enterprise procurement cycle?
7. Can required data be accessed with acceptable privacy anxiety?
8. Does the user return for a second use without heavy researcher prompting?
9. Is there an economically plausible willingness-to-pay path?
10. Can the segment be reached through at least one repeatable channel?

## Evidence upgrade rule

- **Low:** synthetic fixture or single real source.
- **Medium:** 3–5 independent real sources in one coherent segment, with repeated trigger/workaround evidence.
- **High:** repeated qualitative evidence plus observed behaviour in concierge/prototype use and a retention/willingness-to-pay signal.

## Anti-ICP

Deprioritize:

- users who only need a lightweight task list;
- organizations seeking employee surveillance;
- users unwilling to connect any source required for context reconstruction;
- buyers demanding full project-management replacement in v0.1;
- segments where the job occurs rarely and has no meaningful consequence.
