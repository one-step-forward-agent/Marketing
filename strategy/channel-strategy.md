# Channel strategy v0.1 — pre-PMF

## Objective

At the current stage channels are used to **recruit qualified research participants and concierge users**, not to maximize impressions. A channel is valuable if it produces people who actually experience one of the three JTBD with a recent trigger and current workaround.

## Channel priority

| Channel | Job now | Measurement | Scale condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founder / product communities | recruit interviews for context + decisions | qualified conversations / outreach | repeatable segment signal |
| Targeted LinkedIn outreach | reach PM/founder/consultant hypotheses | reply → interview → concierge conversion | message/segment fit |
| Professional network referrals | high-trust interview recruitment | qualified referral rate | referrals repeat beyond founder network |
| Product-management communities/newsletters | research distribution | interview applicants with recent episode | >30% applicants qualify |
| Searchable problem content | capture problem-aware demand | qualified CTA conversion | content gets recurring non-branded demand |
| Shareable research insights | test resonance/language | qualified conversations per 1k views | repeated signal without sensational claims |
| Paid ads | controlled message test only | qualified interview/pilot CPA | retention/value loop exists |

## Channel qualification

A channel should not be called "working" because it generates traffic. It must produce:

1. target-segment people;
2. recent problem episode;
3. real workaround;
4. willingness to spend time on interview or prototype;
5. downstream activation/return behaviour.

## Initial outreach hypotheses

### Context restoration
Target people with 3+ concurrent initiatives. Lead with the observed situation: returning to a project after several days and reconstructing what changed.

### Decision support
Target roles making frequent ambiguous decisions with multiple stakeholders/options. Lead with preserving explicit criteria and rationale, not "AI tells you what to do".

### Time feedback
Target self-directed professionals already using calendar/time tools. Lead with priority alignment, not surveillance/productivity scoring.

## Channel experiment log

For each experiment record:

- channel;
- segment;
- message hypothesis;
- outreach count / visitors;
- qualified conversations;
- interviews completed;
- concierge starts;
- second-use rate;
- objections;
- cost and founder time;
- decision: expand / revise / stop.

## Kill rules

Stop or revise a channel when it repeatedly produces curiosity without qualified episodes, requires misleading claims to convert, or has a cost structure that is incompatible with the validated willingness to pay.
