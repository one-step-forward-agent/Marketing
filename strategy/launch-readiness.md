# Launch readiness gates

A launch is a **season of validated learning and adoption**, not a one-day announcement. This checklist defines when the product may move from research to limited pilot, beta and broader launch.

## Gate 0 — research integrity

- synthetic fixtures clearly labeled;
- real interviews stored separately with consent/provenance;
- one coherent segment is defined;
- no fake testimonials or market statistics.

## Gate 1 — problem evidence

Required:
- ≥5 independent real episodes in one segment for the same job/trigger;
- current workaround visible in most cases;
- concrete consequence/cost;
- at least one contradiction/negative case documented;
- problem occurs often enough to support recurring use.

## Gate 2 — concierge value

Required:
- real cases completed with users;
- user receives output without researcher fabricating evidence;
- measurable or observable improvement;
- correction burden is acceptable;
- at least some users request/repeat the workflow.

## Gate 3 — product activation

Required:
- self-serve or lightly assisted first-value path;
- activation event defined;
- privacy/data access explained before connection;
- error/empty/unsupported states exist;
- first-value time is measured.

## Gate 4 — retention

Required:
- second-use / week-2 behaviour for the validated workflow;
- cohort data by segment and acquisition source;
- no retention story built from researcher reminders alone;
- user can explain why they returned.

## Gate 5 — paid pilot

Required:
- explicit willingness-to-pay evidence;
- price/packaging hypothesis;
- support burden understood;
- basic security/data controls;
- unit-economics instrumentation for LLM/API cost.

## Gate 6 — broader launch

Required:
- repeatable acquisition source;
- onboarding works for non-interview participants;
- product instrumentation and observability;
- incident/rollback path;
- legal/privacy terms appropriate to connected work data;
- customer support owner/process.

## Current state

The project is currently **Gate 0 / early Gate 1 preparation** because the available 30-interview corpus is synthetic. The correct next milestone is real problem evidence, not a public growth launch.
