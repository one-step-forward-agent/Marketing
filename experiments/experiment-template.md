# Marketing / Product experiment template

## Experiment ID
`MXXX` / `HXXX`

## Decision this experiment informs
What decision will change based on the result?

## Segment
One coherent target segment. Do not combine segments in the primary analysis.

## Trigger / situation
Describe the concrete situation in which the job occurs.

## Evidence behind the hypothesis
List source IDs and distinguish empirical vs synthetic evidence.

## Hypothesis
If [intervention/message/workflow] is offered to [segment] during [trigger], then [observable behaviour] will change because [mechanism].

## Primary metric
One primary metric only.

## Pre-registered threshold
Define pass / inconclusive / fail before running the experiment.

## Guardrail metrics
Privacy objection, correction burden, setup time, unsupported recommendation rate, etc.

## Sample / exposure plan
How many qualified participants/exposures are needed for the intended decision? State when the sample is qualitative rather than statistical.

## Procedure
Exact steps so another researcher/operator can reproduce the test.

## Negative evidence to capture
What observations would weaken the hypothesis?

## Result
Raw result and evidence links. Do not rewrite the threshold after seeing the data.

## Decision
- expand
- iterate
- narrow segment
- stop
- return to research

## Follow-up uncertainty
What is the highest-risk unknown remaining?
