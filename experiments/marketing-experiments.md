# Marketing experiment backlog

| ID | Hypothesis | Test | Primary metric | Kill / revise criterion |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| M02 | source traceability increases trust in AI recap | compare recap with vs without source links | preference + correction rate | no trust/usefulness difference |

| M06 | privacy transparency reduces adoption anxiety | show data-control explainer before connect | connect completion + objections | explainer lowers completion / does not change trust objections |
| M07 | PM/founder segment has stronger pull than broad knowledge workers | matched interview batches | frequency × intensity × workaround | no segment separation after 10+ real interviews each |
| M08 | research-led content recruits better users than generic productivity content | 4-post pilot | qualified conversations per 100 visitors | no quality uplift after 4 pieces |

## Experiment discipline

Each experiment must record: segment, trigger, source evidence, expected behavior, pre-declared success threshold, result, decision, and next uncertainty. Do not retroactively redefine success after seeing the data.
