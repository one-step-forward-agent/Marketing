# Research gaps — what must be learned next

## Highest-risk unknowns

1. **Which JTBD is actually recurrent?** The synthetic corpus is exactly 10/10/10 by design.
2. **Which segment feels the strongest cost?** Current persona distribution is also balanced by construction.
3. **What event triggers search/change?** Need concrete recent switching episodes.
4. **How expensive is the current workaround?** Capture minutes, missed decisions, rework, or emotional cost.
5. **What is the real alternative set?** Notes/tasks, generic LLM, Motion, Rize/RescueTime, colleagues, doing nothing, etc.
6. **What data access will users permit?** Privacy acceptance may define the viable product boundary.
7. **What outputs are trusted?** Need evidence on source links, confidence labels, correction workflows.
8. **Which workflow repeats without prompting?** Context recap, decision review, or weekly time review.
9. **Will anyone pay before a broad platform exists?** Test concierge/pilot willingness to pay.
10. **What language do users use unprompted?** Current wording is synthetic and unusable as VOC proof.

## Recommended next sample

Start with 15–20 real interviews across no more than two segments. Do not enforce equal JTBD quotas; let actual episodes reveal the distribution.
